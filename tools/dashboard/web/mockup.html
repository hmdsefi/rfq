Designed, not built (P6) The engine rejects more than one leg today. This is the design for "leg 2 of 3 fails after leg 1 matched".

1. Before the deadline

Leg 1: MM-A's quote is Active and holds. Leg 2: no quote. Leg 3: MM-C's quote is Active and holds. The request is Open. Nothing is escrowed.

2. The deadline fires

Leg 2 has no eligible quote, so the whole request is Expired. MM-A's, MM-C's and the requester's holds are all released in that one command.

3. Alternative path

Every leg has a quote and the request is Presented. MM-B's cancel on leg 2 is rejected because selected quotes are firm. Accept fills all three legs in one command; Reject or a lapse releases all of them.
